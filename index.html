About This App

AKSU Smart Hub is a free academic platform built specifically for students of Akwa Ibom State University (AKSU). It helps you prepare for GST exams, get AI-powered academic assistance, and track your progress — all from your phone.

Features
GST Mock CBT
Practice GST 111, 112, 113, 121, 211, 222, 223 with real timed tests and instant scoring.
AI Academic Assistant
Get help with research methodology, project writing, referencing and academic topics.
Performance Tracking
Track your test scores, best performance and improvement over time.
Secure Accounts
Your data is safe with encrypted passwords and private student accounts.
Mobile Friendly
Designed for your phone — works perfectly on any Android or iPhone.
Available GST Courses
GST 111 — Use of English I GST 112 — Nigerian Peoples & Culture GST 113 — Logic & Philosophy GST 121 — Communication in English GST 211 — History & Philosophy of Science GST 222 — Entrepreneurship GST 223 — Environment & Sustainable Development
